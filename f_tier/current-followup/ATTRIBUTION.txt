This folder contains a modified, approximate reconstruction of the visible
axisymmetric outer profile in native frame 225 (PTS 7.5075 seconds), and an
annotated scientific comparison figure. Those source-derived assets and figures
are distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0. No endorsement by the source author is implied.

Changes: approximate manual silhouette annotation; declared 100 mm height,
1.5 mm wall, 2 mm floor, 80 g mass; revolving the profile into a hollow mesh;
convex collision pieces; approximate transparent preview material. These are
local simulation assumptions, not calibrated source measurements. The asset
is not original GRID CAD and does not establish native physics or A06 acceptance.
